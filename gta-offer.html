Questions

THINGS PEOPLE Always Ask

I'm a complete beginner, is this for me?

Absolutely. Every plan comes in beginner, intermediate and advanced levels, and the education library covers fundamentals through to advanced race strategy. Michael came in as a total beginner and finished his first Half Ironman. You'll find your level and build from there.

What's the difference between the Academy and 1:1 coaching?

With 1:1 coaching you get a fully personalised weekly training plan, two dedicated check-ins per week, and bespoke programming at £199 per month. The Academy gives you knowledge, community, expert coaching access, and a structured plan at a fraction of that cost. Many members use the Academy as a stepping stone and upgrade to 1:1 when they're ready.

What if I decide I want 1:1 coaching after joining?

No problem and no penalty. If within your first month you decide to move to 1:1 coaching at £199/month, we'll deduct your first month's coaching fee from the Academy cost. You won't be out of pocket.

What is Discord and do I need to download anything?

Discord is a free messaging platform on your phone, tablet or laptop. It takes about two minutes to set up. Think of it as a private, members-only group built for supportive, like-minded athletes. If you can use WhatsApp, you can use Discord.

How do the group coaching calls work?

Twice a month, Garth runs a live group coaching call inside the Discord Inner Circle. Topics are driven by popular demand. Members vote on what matters most and Garth coaches on it live. If you can't make it, every call is recorded and available within 24 hours.

Can I switch training plans during the year?

Yes. Unlimited access means you're not locked into one distance. Move from Sprint to Olympic, add a 70.3 block, whatever your season needs. Message Garth and he'll load the new plan into TrainingPeaks.

What happens after the first 50 members?

The price goes to £799/year. Founding members lock in £499 permanently, and that's your price for life as long as you stay a member. We don't grandfather in new members at the old rate once the 50 spots are gone.
